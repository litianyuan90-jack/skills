无尽灯The Inexhaustible Lamp

譬如一灯燃百千灯，冥者皆明，明终不尽。
——《维摩诘所说经 · 菩萨品》

“It is like one lamp lighting a hundred thousand lamps: the dark is all made bright, and the brightness is never used up.” — Vimalakīrti Sūtra

镜里的这盏灯，你数不尽。灯可以传下去，智能体也可以——让它们在云端长明，灯灯相续。The lamp in the mirror cannot be counted. A lamp can be passed on, and so can an agent: let yours keep burning in the cloud, lamp to lamp.

照見The Mirror Cave

一座以莫高窟“覆斗顶”形制为原型的镜窟。西壁佛龛不塑佛像，只立一面铜镜；入洞之后，来路亦化为镜。两镜相照，窟中有窟，千窟千佛。镜中每深一层，时间便早一步；镜中每隔一层，色与空便互换一次。你在镜中找不到自己。

A cave modelled on the truncated-pyramid ceiling chambers of Mogao. The niche of the west wall holds no statue, only a bronze mirror; once you are inside, the doorway behind you becomes a mirror too. Two mirrors facing each other: caves within caves, a thousand caves, a thousand Buddhas. Each layer deeper into the mirror is a little earlier in time; every other layer swaps form for emptiness. You will not find yourself in it.

如何观看 · HOW TO LOOK

  • 拖动环顾，W/S 或 ↑/↓ 前后行走 · Drag to look; W/S or ↑/↓ to walk
  • 滚轮或右侧时间轴：起稿 → 敷彩 → 剥落 → 空 → 再生 · Scroll or use the rail to move time: sketch → colour → decay → emptiness → again
  • 在镜上拖动，拂拭尘埃（神秀）；然后静止不动（惠能） · Drag on the mirror to wipe it (Shenxiu); then keep still (Huineng)
  • 色 ⇄ 空：交换镜内外的色与空 · Swap form and emptiness inside and outside the mirror
  • 那盏灯 · Touch the lamp
